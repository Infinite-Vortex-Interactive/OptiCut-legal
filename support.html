Common questions

How do I enter fractions? Type them directly — e.g. 3/4, or mixed numbers like 2 3/8.

What does “grain / pattern” do? It locks each cut so its long side runs with the sheet’s grain (the sheet’s height), and disables rotation.

Does OptiCut work offline? Yes — it runs entirely on your device, with no account and no internet connection required.
