OptiCut — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 July 2026

OptiCut (“the app”) is a sheet-cut optimizer. This policy explains how the app handles your information. In short: it doesn’t collect any.

Information we collect

None. OptiCut does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal or usage data. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers.

How your data is used

Everything you enter — sheet sizes, cut lists, and settings — is used only to calculate a cutting layout on your device, and is not sent anywhere. The app works fully offline; it makes no network requests. All fonts and assets are bundled inside the app.

Permissions

The Android build declares the INTERNET permission (a standard part of the app framework it is built on), but the app makes no network connections.

Children’s privacy

OptiCut does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new “last updated” date.
